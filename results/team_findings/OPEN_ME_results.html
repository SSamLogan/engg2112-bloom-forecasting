ENGG2112: Cross-lake bloom forecasting

Goal: assess whether models trained on other lakes can predict bloom conditions during an upcoming ten-day period at an unseen lake.

Each experiment held out one entire lake for testing. Validation used the latest available year from each of the remaining lakes; earlier years were used for training.

The “held_out_lake” column identifies the experiment. In a validation table, the results concern the other training lakes, not the held-out lake.

Satellite images were available only for Erie. For the other three held-out lakes, the combined model used images during training but received no images at prediction time.
